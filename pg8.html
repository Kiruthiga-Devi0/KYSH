Quick answers

How can I pay?

Choose Cash on Delivery and pay when your order arrives, or pay online by UPI, card or netbanking through Razorpay.

Where can I see my orders?

Log in and open Orders in the top bar. Every order you've placed is listed there.

How do I use a coupon code?

Enter the code in your bag before you check out. For example, KYSH10 takes 10% off (up to ₹300) on orders above ₹999.

Which sizes are available?

S, M, L, XL and XXL. Pick your size on the product page before adding to your bag.
